#### Additional validation

#### Additional validation

- On an OIDC deployment, repeated authorization checks do not refetch discovery or JWKS on every call.
- Studio direct OIDC login creates the email-prefix workspace when authorization allows it, and does not create it when authorization denies it.

#### Draft release-note text

Setup prefers OpenRouter among detected keys, and `NVIDIA_API_KEY` alone no longer auto-creates a provider. Authentication caches IdP discovery and signing keys.

**Description**

Automodel, Unsloth, and RL training steps support cooperative pause with a checkpoint, checkpoint-backed resume, and rerun after failure. W&B and MLflow runs continue across recovery.

**User impact**

Users can free GPUs without losing progress and can recover failed runs. Paused-job storage is kept for 7 days by default, up to a 30-day maximum. Failed-job storage is kept for four hours.

#### CLI validation

```sh
nemo jobs list --workspace default --filter.source customization --filter.status error
nemo jobs pause automodel-a1b2c3d4e5f6 --workspace default
nemo jobs resume automodel-a1b2c3d4e5f6 --workspace default
nemo jobs control automodel-a1b2c3d4e5f6 --workspace default --pause-ttl-seconds 1209600
nemo jobs rerun automodel-a1b2c3d4e5f6 --workspace default
```

Expected:

- Pause saves a checkpoint and releases the GPUs. Resume continues from that checkpoint, and metrics continue from where they stopped.
- Rerun of an `error` job starts a new attempt from the newest checkpoint. The failed attempt stays in `nemo jobs attempts list`, and the W&B or MLflow run continues.

#### Additional validation

- Resume that finds no checkpoint fails and does not start over (#3301).
- Pausing a pod that is waiting for GPUs pauses immediately (#3416).
- Changing the training image while a job is paused produces an `image_digest_warning`.
- After storage expires, recovery is refused.
- Repeat on the Docker, subprocess, Kubernetes, and Volcano backends.

#### Draft release-note text

Automodel, Unsloth, and RL training steps support cooperative pause, checkpoint-backed resume, and rerun after failure, with W&B and MLflow continuity.
